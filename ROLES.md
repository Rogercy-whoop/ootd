# Team and roles

**Team 11**, a team of three, launched OOTD ("outfit of the day") as its startup at LaunchX, an entrepreneurship programme at Boston University, in summer 2025, and presented it at demo day.

| Member | Main role |
|---|---|
| **Yu Chen (Roger)** | Team leader and technical lead |
| Eileen | Marketing and outlets |
| Quinten | Brand name design and financial analysis |

The roles overlapped: each of us also worked on the others' parts.

## My part (Roger)

- **Team leader.** I led Team 11 and, like everyone, also contributed to the brand, marketing and finance work.
- **Technical lead, July 2025.** I built the app's interface by directing AI coding tools (Firebase Studio and Cursor), integrated the AI models and their APIs (Google Gemini to tag clothing photos and suggest outfits, and Replicate to remove photo backgrounds), and set up the Firebase project and its hosting. Free users were limited to five AI-analysed closet items. The app ran as a prototype.
- **Relaunch, October 2026.** I decided what to keep, cut and fix, made the app guest-only, capped free use at five photo analyses per browser, removed the unfinished payment flow, and put the web app online on Netlify, with Claude Code writing the code changes.
