# Team and roles

OOTD ("outfit of the day") was the company of **Team 11**, a team of three, at LaunchX, an entrepreneurship programme at Boston University, in summer 2025.

| Member | Main role |
|---|---|
| **Yu Chen (Roger)** | Team leader and technical lead |
| Eileen | Marketing and outlets |
| Quinten | Brand name design and financial analysis |

The roles overlapped: each of us also worked on the others' parts.

## My part (Roger)

- **Team leader.** I led Team 11 and, like everyone, also contributed to the brand, marketing and finance work.
- **Technical lead, July 2025.** I built the app's interface by directing AI coding tools (Firebase Studio and Cursor), integrated the AI models and their APIs (Google Gemini to tag clothing photos and suggest outfits, and Replicate to remove photo backgrounds), and set up the Firebase project and its hosting. The app itself was not deployed that summer.
- **Relaunch, October 2026.** I decided what to keep, cut and fix, made the app guest-only and removed the unfinished payment flow, and put it online on Netlify, with Claude Code writing the code changes.
